A reward tracker made with love
💙 Made by a dad, for parents
This app was built by an autistic kid's dad, with love. It's completely free for any parent who might find it useful. If it helps even one family, it was worth every minute building it.
🎯 What it does
Star Chart helps you track your child's rewards and accomplishments across daily activities — meals, activities, social moments, and more.

The idea is simple: show your kid the app together. Watching their circles fill up and their savings grow is genuinely motivating. It worked for my little one — I hope it works for yours too. 🌟
💰 How the earnings work
Every circle your child fills earns them real money — by default, $0.10 per point. The total they accumulate over a period becomes their allowance. It turns good behavior into something tangible, visible, and exciting to work toward.
🔧 Make it yours
Use the Config tab to customize groups, slots, and category labels to fit your child's specific routine and goals. Every kid is different — this app is built to adapt.
🏠 Family Codes
Each family gets their own private space using a family code. Share your code with your partner so you both see the same data in real time. Keep it safe — anyone with your code can access your chart.
